# Ituri top five

The active set combines Ituri relevance with at least 1,500 authentic aligned
pairs. Original high-population candidates without usable bitext remain visible
in the acquisition backlog.

| Rank | Language | ISO | Curated bitext | Publication status |
|---:|---|---|---:|---|
| 1 | Alur / Dho-Alur | `alz` | 7,567 English pairs | Open CC BY 4.0 |
| 2 | Ngiti | `niy` | 7,955 French pairs | Local evaluation only; restricted text |
| 3 | Kakwa | `keo` | 7,950 French pairs | Local evaluation only; restricted text |
| 4 | Komo | `kmw` | 7,938 French pairs | Local evaluation only; restricted text |
| 5 | Amba | `rwm` | 2,074 French pairs | Local evaluation only; restricted text |

All five exceed the minimum. Open Alur data is tracked; restricted raw and
processed text remains local and Git-ignored. Metadata, hashes, counts,
preparation scripts, and permitted future results are published.

Lendu (`led`), Hema (`nix`), and Bila (`bip`) remain high-priority Ituri
acquisition backlogs. Searches across OPUS, Hugging Face, MT560 wrappers,
CLEAR Global, SMOL, FLORES, linguistic resources, and the package found no
usable English/French sentence bitext for them. Future searches continue across
humanitarian, education, health, government, dictionary-example, subtitle, and
community-publication sources—not only religious text.
