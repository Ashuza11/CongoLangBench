# Eastern DRC top-five bitext tracks

This is the first regional top-five selection after the four national-language
tracks. Selection is based on Eastern DRC relevance plus confirmed bitext
availability, not speaker count alone.

| Language | ISO | Confirmed pairs | Publication status |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Nande / Kinande | `nnb` | 14,949 | Public, CC BY 4.0 |
| Mashi / Shi | `shr` | 1,500 | Public, CC BY 4.0; target reached |
| Fuliiru / Kifuliiru | `flr` | 30,546 | Curated locally; restricted text |
| Tembo / Kitembo | `tbt` | 7,932 | Curated locally; restricted text |
| Nyanga / Kinyanga | `nyj` | 3,192 | Curated locally; text kept private |

All five tracks exceed the 1,500-pair target and are ready for the later
benchmark-freeze step. The three package-derived tracks retain their authentic
source text as supplied; raw and processed files remain Git-ignored where
redistribution is not explicit.

Havu is already represented by 3,224 public Havu--French pairs under CC BY-SA
4.0. It is reserved for the later dedicated Kivu expansion rather than counted
among this Eastern regional top five.
