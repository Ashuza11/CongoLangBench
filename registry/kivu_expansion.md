# Dedicated Kivu expansion

The Eastern DRC baseline already contains Nande, Mashi, Fuliiru, Tembo, and
Nyanga. The dedicated Kivu phase adds locally relevant tracks without
duplicating their underlying data when a dataset is already curated in another
regional directory.

| Expansion language | ISO | Curated pairs | Handling |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Havu / Kihavu | `hav` | 3,224 | Open CC BY-SA 4.0 |
| Hunde / Kihunde | `hke` | 2,291 | Restricted local evaluation |
| Lega-Shabunda / Pangi | `lea` | 30,703 | Restricted local; reuse Maniema track |
| Lega-Mwenga | `lgm` | 7,943 | Restricted local; reuse Maniema track |
| Bembe / Kibembe | `bmb` | 2,984 | Restricted local; reuse Maniema track |

All five expansion tracks exceed the 1,500-pair minimum. Together with the
Eastern baseline, they provide ten curated Kivu-relevant language tracks.
Variety and source-region caveats remain mandatory: the two Lega tracks are
separate, and reused DRC editions are not silently relabelled as
territory-specific data.

## Continued acquisition backlog

The expansion remains open to additional authentic bitext. Priority searches
include Nyindu, Vira/Joba, Konzo, Talinga-Bwisi, and other Kivu languages with
confirmed DRC provenance. New candidates must reach 1,500 aligned units before
joining the ready set.
