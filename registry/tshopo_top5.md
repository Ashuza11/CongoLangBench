# Tshopo top five

Tshopo is complete for the current curation phase. The selection prioritizes
languages spoken in the province for which at least 1,500 authentic aligned
units are available. Komo and Ngombe reference already curated datasets rather
than duplicating their text in a second regional directory.

| Rank | Language | ISO | Pairs | Alignment | Repository handling |
|---:|---|---|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Komo / Kumu | `kmw` | 7,938 | French, verse | restricted local; existing track reused |
| 2 | Ngombe | `ngc` | 12,735 | French, verse | open; existing track reused |
| 3 | Kele / Lokele | `khy` | 7,910 | French, verse | restricted local |
| 4 | Lengola | `lej` | 5,774 | English, verse | open CC BY-SA 4.0 |
| 5 | Lombo / Turumbu | `loo` | 1,812 | French, verse | open CC BY-SA 4.0 |

Komo is spoken in the Tshopo transition area, and Ngombe communities are also
present in the province. Their source editions are DRC-labelled rather than
territory-labelled, so that provenance limitation must remain visible in any
regional analysis.

## Acquisition backlog

- Topoke/Poke (`pof`): currently identified open portions yield fewer than
  1,500 alignable verses.
- Mbole (`mdq`): identified resources are audio rather than downloadable text.
- Mituku (`zmq`): the identified open Mark portion is below 1,500 pairs.
- Bali (`bcp`): the public reader exposes only a small set of portions, below
  the benchmark minimum.
- Enya-Kisangani: no independently verified 1,500-pair text source was found;
  it must remain distinct from Enya-Kindu.

The `africa-bitext-builder` inventory was checked first. Open document sites,
OPUS, Hugging Face, ScriptureEarth, and official online editions were then
searched. Only independently verified data is counted.
