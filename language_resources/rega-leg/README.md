# Lega / Rega — keep `leg` and `lea` separate

- **Lega-Mwenga:** ISO 639-3 `leg`, centered on Mwenga.
- **Lega-Shabunda:** ISO 639-3 `lea`, associated with Shabunda/Pangi.

“Kilega,” “Kirega,” and “Rega” are ambiguous. Store the ISO code, locality, speaker origin, and edition with every record; do not merge these varieties without community/linguist validation.

## Verified and evaluated resources

- **CATALOG RECORD — BIBLE/MEDIA (`lea`) — TERMS:** [Scripture Earth Lega-Shabunda](https://www.scriptureearth.org/00eng.php?iso=lea) lists Kilega scripture, songbook, app/media, and JESUS Film. Accessibility does not establish an open bulk-text or audio licence; contact the listed organization.
- **CONVERTIBLE SOURCE — BIBLE — MIXED:** search [eBible Corpus](https://github.com/BibleNLP/ebible), [find.bible](https://find.bible/), and Bible.is separately for `leg` and `lea`. Record editions separately.
- Grammars, ethnographies, songbooks, dictionaries, and archive recordings are **CONTACT LEADS** until files, community consent, and licences are documented.

## Best sources you can use now

1. **DATA — PARALLEL BIBLE APP (`lea`):** [Find.Bible’s *Mikanda Zili mu Idagi* record](https://find.bible/bibles/LEAUFM/) identifies a 2002 full Lega-Shabunda Bible and an Android app with parallel French and Swahili.
2. **DATA — DIRECT APK (`lea`) — TERMS:** [Scripture Earth](https://www.scriptureearth.org/00eng.php?iso=lea) provides a direct Kilega Bible app download. The app supports offline text; obtain publisher permission before extracting or redistributing its database.
3. **DATA — SIDE-BY-SIDE TEXT (`lea`):** the [Kilega Bible app description](https://apkpure.net/bible-in-kilega/org.ipsapps.rdc.lea.bible.lega) states that Kilega can be displayed alongside French *Français courant 97* and Swahili *Toleo Wazi Neno*. This is concrete parallel content, but the French/Swahili editions have their own rights.
4. **DATA — SPEECH (`lea`):** [Global Recordings Network](https://globalrecordings.net/fr/language/4587) offers downloadable Kilega evangelistic audio. Check recording terms and seek transcripts/speaker consent.
5. No similarly concrete source was verified for **Lega-Mwenga `leg`**; do not transfer `lea` data into that track.
