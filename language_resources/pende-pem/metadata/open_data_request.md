# Request for open Pende parallel text

Hello, we are curating a public text-evaluation dataset for languages of the
Democratic Republic of the Congo. Do you know of, or can you share, an openly
licensed Pende/Phende (`pem`) dataset aligned with French or English—especially
humanitarian, education, health, dictionary-example, subtitle, localization,
government, or community-publication text? We need at least 1,500 aligned
sentences and will preserve the source, variety, licence, and attribution.
