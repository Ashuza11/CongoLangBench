# Kanyok text and permission request

## Short email

**Subject:** Research access request for the 2008 Kanyok Bible (`KNYFUL`)

Hello,

I am building CongoLangBitextEval, a non-commercial research project that
documents and evaluates language-model performance on languages spoken in the
Democratic Republic of the Congo. Find.Bible lists the 2008 “Kanyok Bible with
Deuterocanon” under identifier `KNYFUL`, but its page does not provide a text or
provider link.

Could you please identify the publisher/rightsholder and provide, or direct me
to, a digital verse-keyed copy? I also request written permission to:

1. align the Kanyok text with French or English for research and evaluation;
2. publish at least 1,500 aligned sentence or verse pairs in an open dataset;
3. publish model predictions, aggregate metrics, and error analysis; and
4. adapt only formatting needed for alignment, such as removing notes and
   normalizing whitespace.

Please specify the licence, required attribution, permitted repository, and any
non-commercial or redistribution restrictions. We will preserve the edition,
publisher, copyright, and attribution metadata exactly as requested.

Thank you.

## Contact routes

- Digital Bible Society: https://dbs.org/
- Find.Bible catalogue: https://find.bible/bibles/KNYFUL/
- ACTB-RDC contact: https://actbrdc.org/en/contact

The request should be sent to the catalogue maintainer first, asking them to
identify the actual publisher/rightsholder. Do not interpret a catalogue reply
or access grant as redistribution permission unless publication of derived
aligned text is explicitly allowed.
