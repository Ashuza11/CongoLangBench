# Kanyok source inventory

## Confirmed language identity

- Language: Kanyok / Kanyoka / Kanioka
- ISO 639-3: `kny`
- Country: Democratic Republic of the Congo
- Project region: Kasai--Lomami
- Population-oriented estimate: approximately 582,000 DRC speakers (Joshua
  Project); Find.Bible lists 200,000. The disagreement is retained rather than
  presenting either value as definitive.

## Located text lead

- Title: Kanyok Bible with Deuterocanon
- Catalogue identifier: `KNYFUL`
- Date: 2008
- Catalogue: https://find.bible/bibles/KNYFUL/
- Catalogue maintainer: Digital Bible Society / Forum of Bible Agencies
- Digital text: not exposed by the catalogue
- Licence: not stated
- Redistribution: not authorized
- Status checked: 2026-08-23

The catalogue confirms that this is an indigenous DRC Kanyok resource but
contains no outbound provider or download link. Searches of the installed
`africa-bitext-builder` registry found no `kny` entry. Searches for open Kanyok,
Kanyoka, and `KNYFUL` corpora found no downloadable parallel dataset.

Digital Bible Society explains that its libraries may be copied and shared in
their supplied form, but content should not be uploaded to the internet or
modified without written consent. Therefore catalogue discovery or library
access alone is not sufficient permission to publish a derived bitext dataset.

If the text is supplied for local research only, align it to an authorized
French or English Bible by verse key and keep all generated text Git-ignored.
Only source metadata, scripts, counts, and evaluation results may be published
until redistribution permission is explicit.
