# Kanyok source inventory

## Confirmed language identity

- Language: Kanyok / Kanyoka / Kanioka
- ISO 639-3: `kny`
- Country: Democratic Republic of the Congo
- Project region: Kasai--Lomami
- Population-oriented estimate: approximately 582,000 DRC speakers (Joshua
  Project); Find.Bible lists 200,000. The disagreement is retained rather than
  presenting either value as definitive.

## Located text leads

- Title: Kanyok Bible books on jw.org
- Site path: `https://www.jw.org/kny/cilamin-cya-mikand/bibl/nwt/books/`
- Site language path: `https://www.jw.org/kny/`
- Book slugs found: `matewus`, `mark`, `luuk`, `jah`, `myand-yilongel-bapostol`,
  `tiit`
- Status checked: 2026-08-25

The jw.org Kanyok pages expose verse-keyed book chapters. The completed local
workflow extracted 4,808 source verses and retained 4,788 unique aligned
French--Kanyok pairs. This supersedes the earlier assumption that Kanyok had no
digital text and completes its 1,500-pair acquisition target.

The source is still not treated as open-licence material. Keep any derived text
Git-ignored unless redistribution terms are explicit. Only source metadata,
scripts, counts, checksums, and evaluation results may be published until
redistribution permission is explicit.
