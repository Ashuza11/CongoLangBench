# Tembo / Kitembo / Chitembo (`tbt`)

Search all three names plus `tbt`, DRC, and Kivu; “Tembo” alone is ambiguous.

## Verified and evaluated resources

- **CATALOG RECORD — BIBLE — TERMS:** [YouVersion Tembo](https://www.bible.com/languages/tbt) lists *Echilaano Chiyayaya* from Wycliffe. Reading/app availability is not an open corpus licence.
- **CATALOG RECORD — BIBLE/MEDIA:** [find.bible Tembo](https://find.bible/languages/tbt/) lists Bible editions, Words of Life, and JESUS Film. Its catalog export is metadata, not the underlying text/audio.
- **CONVERTIBLE SOURCE — ORTHOGRAPHY/TEXT SAMPLE:** [ScriptSource Tembo](https://scriptsource.org/cms/scripts/page.php?item_id=language_detail&key=tbt) verifies `tbt`, names, Latin writing system, keyboard, and references. It is not a corpus.
- **DATA — ONLINE DICTIONARY/TRANSLATION MEMORY:** [Glosbe Tembo–French](https://fr.glosbe.com/tbt/fr) exposes a `tbt` dictionary interface and may show translated examples. Verify that individual entries actually exist and are Tembo; Glosbe access does not grant bulk extraction rights.

## Best sources you can use now

1. Read/search the [YouVersion Tembo edition](https://www.bible.com/languages/tbt) and record its exact publisher/version.
2. Use Glosbe for manual Tembo–French lookup and collect a list of promising entries for speaker verification, not automated copying.
3. Ask Wycliffe/the YouVersion publisher for verse-keyed USFM and explicit research permission.
4. Use ScriptSource’s keyboard and orthography references to normalize future text without silently removing vowel length or diacritics.
