# Sanga source inventory

- Language: Sanga / Kisanga / Luba-Sanga
- ISO 639-3: `sng`
- Glottocode: `sang1331`
- Region: Southeastern Democratic Republic of the Congo, especially Katanga
- Investigation date: 2026-08-22
- Current publishable pairs: 0
- Target: at least 1,500 pairs

## Findings

1. [Glottolog](https://glottolog.org/resource/languoid/id/sang1331) confirms
   Sanga (`sng`) as a language of the Democratic Republic of the Congo and
   distinguishes it from related languages.
2. [Find a Bible](https://find.bible/languages/sng/) records Kisanga Bible
   portions and identifies Sanga as a DRC language.
3. [Scripture Earth](https://www.scriptureearth.org/00eng.php?iso=sng) exposes
   the Kisanga JESUS film but no downloadable text corpus.
4. The Trinitarian Bible Society reports a 1991 Kisanga Bible and an ongoing
   revision project. Its public project page does not grant dataset
   redistribution rights.
5. `africa-bitext-builder` 0.1.13 resolves `sng` to no language entry or Bible
   version. `sag` is Sango and is not a valid substitute.

## Acquisition decision

Keep Sanga selected, but mark it `source_acquisition_required`. Contact the
Trinitarian Bible Society or another documented rightsholder for a digital
verse-keyed edition and explicit permission to redistribute an aligned
French--Sanga or English--Sanga dataset. If an independently licensed open
source is found, prefer it and retain every usable aligned pair.
