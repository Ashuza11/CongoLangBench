Luka

11Baito likam baosila okota oemalisa bombila loka lo mbeko yeke itomoaka loka liso, 2kee beke bakaenaka ta baiso bibo oakola ta liako, beke bakasona bakeleli ba lioi, bakafesu yo.3Kolaongoma, Teofili,butu a lokuku, mbisa yange iyolinga linga litina li ye mbeko itotina, ei aleko lokaenyela mbo losonelowe loksa loka loyamo itotina. 4Kolaongoma otolembela lamba mbeko ikaekisomo lao imbe omongo wewe.5Tambile eya Elode bokata bo Yuda, imbaka la kanga emoi, lina linde mbo Yekalia, buto a kalasi eya Abia. Boali inde ambaka buto a bofindi boya Alona, lina linde mbo Elisabeta. Bo abaele bambaka baengani ta baiso baya Mungu, bambaka batokenda la tokpelo la tokela toya Ngene ndoena bobe. 7Baitimba la wana loka mbo Elisabeta ambaka bokomba; la bo atotina bambaka batolo.8Lisiyo limoi ta lisangi li kalasi inde, Yekalia ambaka atokela likua linde li kanga felo iya Mungu, 9ta bofono bo bokanga, la ikaenganela ye mbo aote ta eseke lotanu loya Ngene otumba bobele bo liesa. 10Likama li baito limbaka lousa batosembola ta mbile oetumba bobele bo liesa.11Bokitomo boya Ngene akaenamana linde ta etumbelo lo bobele ta boo bolome. 12Ekaenaka Yekalia ongoma, kabanga la bowa bokaotela ye. 13Ndjo Bokitomo akabeka linde mbo, oibangaka, Yekalia, loka mbo lisemboli liao lifokana, la boali ao Elisabeta atobotelo we wanabotolome, la otota ye la lina mbo Yoani.14Liomali la limengo lifi litomba lao, la baito likama batooka limengo ta libotomu linde. 15Loka mbo atomba butu bofi ta baiso baya Ngene, aitomwa baana, lamba leka lo lewisa; la atowala la Bolimo Botanu kota solo eya inango inde.16Atobinoisa baito ba Isalaele likama ambe Ngene Mungu ibo. 17Ye aleko atoka felo inde la bolimo la bofoka boya Eliya. Atobinoisa betema be ba ise ambe bana, la baito ba tale ta ndole e liengani, loka lokomanyela Ngene bofindi boengani.18Yekalioa akabeka la Bokitomo mbo, loto lembela yeke isani? loka mbo loomba botolo, la boali ange aleko amba inunu. 19Bokitomo akabinoisa bonoko mbo, Ei Gabaliele, ono atoemala felo iya Mungu, la lotomomo oya beka lao la otondolelo we ye mbeko. 20Ndjo ko kanikani otomba ebebe, oito beka kota lisiyo lieke litotomoa ye mbeko, loka oikaimila baoi bange, beke batoengana ta elaka lamo.21Baito baumba kela Yekalia la bakamwala mbo aokolo ta eseke lotanu, 22Ndjo oko etomoaka ye aikaluwa obekana libo, la baka lembela mbo aene lienesi ta eseke lotanu.Ye aleko aka mbaka atofefia bo ndembe ndjo akatikala ebebe. 23Oko esilaka basiyo binde balikuwa, akaka ta mbele inde.24Mbisa i basiyo be boali inde Elisabeta akanya lieme, la akaisa tengai liondo. Akabeka mbo, 25Ngene aosila okelelei ani ta basiyo be,oko akamoilekei, loka lofoya soni yange ta baito.26Ta tengai eliambe, bokitimo Gabaliele aka tomomo la Mungu oka ta boki bomoi bo Gallilai, la lina liko Nasalete, 27ambe boseka bomoi ono aitoka luwa botolome ta bopfindi boya Dawidi. Lina liya bolome mbo Yosefe, la lina liya botaali Malia. 28Akaota elo embe ye, la akabeka mbo kalibo! botaali a ngbengbele, Ngene ambo lao. 29Ndjo akabamba la bowa ta bonoko wamo, la akamba la toloi lamba kalibo amo ilangi obeka isai.30Bokitomo akabeka linde mbo, Ndo banga, Malia, loka mbo ofoanya ngbengbele ambe Mungu. 31Tene, otoanya lieme,la otobota wana botolome, la otota ye lina mbo Yesu. 32Ye atomba bofi la atotomo mbo wana aya ono Aeti likolo. Ngene Mungu atofa ye kiti ebokota eya Dawidi ise inde. 33Atomba bokota ta mbele eya Yakobo omongo, la bokota winde boitomba la tunga.34Malia akabeka la bokitomo mbo, yeke itomba isai eke eitaka lalei la botolome? 35Bokitomo aka binoisela ye mbo,Bolimo Botanu atoya liokolo liao, la bofoka boya Ono-Aeti likolo itoufo we. Yamo yoko wana botanu ono ato botomo atotomo wana aya Mungu.36Tene Elisabeta boboto wao, ye aleko afoanya lieme li wana botolome ta botoolo winde, la tengai ene embe tengai inde ee liambe ono atomoko mbo aitobota. 37Loka mbo ambe Mungu mbeko itotina iyengani okelomo. 38Maliya akabeka mbo, tene, ei bokowa boya Ngene, tikaka mbo ikomelei kani ekaosila obekowe. La bokitomo akatika ye akaka.39Ta mbile amo Maliya akaemala la akaka mangu ta boki bomoi ta longo lo Yuda. 40Akaota ta mbele eya Jekaliya, la akafa Elisabeta kalibo. 41Ekaokaka Elisabeta kalibo eya Maliya, wana ta soolo inde akatila, la Elisabeta akawalisomo la Bolimo Botanu,42akamama la limiki lifi mbo, Owe ofoutchelomo ta bataali la wana ta solo ao afoutchelomo. 43Loka loingi lobeko leke lo'otomoelei mbo inango aya Ngene ange aoya ambei? 44Tene, oko ekaokana limiki li kalibo ao katikati ta batoi bange, wana kati ee solo ange aotila la liomali. 45Liomali ta botaali omo akaimilomo, loka mbo baoi bakabeka Ngene inde bafoenganisomo!46Maliya akabeka mbo, Liwawi liange liosimola Ngene, 47bolimo wange bofo'omala la Mungu Bouwesi wange.48Loka mbo aomoila boeno bo lokuku boya bokowa winde, tene koakola kanikani befindi betotina batotangei mbo buto a limengo. 49Loka mbo Ono aeti Bofi aokelelei mbeko ifi fafo, la lina linde limbe botanu.50Ngandi inde imbe loka lo beke batobanga ye, befindi la befindi. 51Ta boo winde akakela bofoka, aomelanisa baito beke bakaenyelisa baenyeli ba lofolo ta betema bibo,52la aofoya baito ba bofoka ta keka ee bokota, aikalilisa baito ba lokuku. 53Aowalisa baito la semba ta biongo bilo, la aobinoisa baito ba lifoka baoo bule.54Aosunga Isalaele bokuwi winde, afoenyelisa ngandi inde, 55(ani ebekaka ye la bangau biso) loka loya Abalayama la bofindi winde layalaya.56Malia akaumoa linde eseke lomoi lamba tengai isaso, la akabinoa ta mbele inde. 57Lisangi liya Elisabeta obota wana likaengana, la akabota wana botolome. 58Kendesani linde la befindi binde bakaoka mbo Ngene afokela ye ngandi fafo, la bakaomala linde.59Mbisa ibasiyo bonanei bakatena wana itombe, la bakakola ota ye mbo Yekalia, kee ani embe lina liya ise inde.60Ndjo inango inde akabinoisa bonoko mbo, tingalo,atotomo mbo Yoani.62Baka kelela ise inde ndembe lamba alangi ota wana la lini lina. 63Akalengila lombao losonela, la akasona mbo lina linde mbo Yoani.64La bonoko winde la lolame linde bokalikoa la mangu, la akabeka, akasimola Mungu. 65Bowa bokamba libo atotina beke bambaka onga, la mbeko yeke itotina imbaka bo bato beka ta boki bo longo loto tina lo Yuda. 66Batotina beke bakao kaka yo bakaisa yo ta betema bibo, obeka mbo, kolaongoma, wana ono atoma ndai? Loka mbo wewe boo boya Ngene lombaka linde.67Yekalia ise inde aka walisomo la Bolimo Botanu, la akanagola mbo, 68Ngene Mungu a Isalaele asimolo moko loka mbo aomoila baiti binde la aokomanya liendolomu libo.69Aotomelesu bouwesi bofoka ta mbele eya Dawidi bokowa winde. 70Kanda emoi ani ebekaka ye koala ta benoko be betondoli binde batanu mbo, 71atouwise su ta beke batonanise su la ta bao ba baiyani biso batotina, la aleko mbo.72Atokela bakuwa ba ngandi ta bangau biso, la atoenyelisa liokamani linde litanu, 73Ko lolapi leke atenaka ambe Abalayama ngau iso. 74Mbo tofoyomo ta bofoka bo baiyani biso loka kembo tokuwele ye ndoena bowa. 75La botanu la liengani felo inde bassiyo biso batotina.76Owe Wana, ombo tomoko bonangoli boya Ono Aeti likolo, loka kembo optoka felo iya Ngene okomanya ndole inde,77oluwisa baito binde loka lo liwisomu la lomesilomo lo bobe wibo.78Loka lo botema bo ngandi boya Mungu iso. La ngandi yamo, buse botondjele esu la bose, 79Loka lo liangangi li beke bangali ta lotiyo, la katikati i lilima li lingba, la loka lokendisa bkolo biso ta ndole ekimia.80Wana akakonda, la akanya bofoka ta Bolimo, la akangala ta liombele tii kota lisiyo linde liyoenena ta Isalaele.
21Ta elo mbile, elo Kaisala Ogisito, akata ekela mbo baito batotina ba wenda basonomo baina ta kasa mbo batangomo loketi libo. 2Lotangomo lo felo lotangomoko ani Kuliniuv ambe bokota bo yese iko Sulio, 3batotina bakaka okasonomo baina, buto la buto la boki winde.4Yosefu aleko alilaka ta Galilaya, ta boki bo Nasaleta, loka bake ta Yudeya, ta boki boya Tawiti, bokumo Beteleyeme, loka ambaka buto a bofindi boya Tawiti. 5Mbo ake okasonomo la Maliya baemo binde ono ambaka la lieme,6ta mbile embaka onga, mbile ee yelo lo'lobota loya Maliya lokaengana. 7La akabota wana inde afelofelo botolome, akalingila ye la bolekani, akalalisa ye ta wato, loka elela loitimba ta mbele ee beenda.8Imbaka, la batchungani ba bekoko balalaka lousa ta bilanga lotiyo loka lo lomoilo bekoko. 9La tene, bokitomo boya Ngene bokatomoela bo futuku, la liangangi liya Ngene likalingila bo.10Kola ongoma bokitomo akabeka libo mbo: Oibangakai; loka lo yele'nu mboli itanu, iyaii, ye itomba loka lo baito batotina; liomali lifi. 11La boni, ta boki boya Tawiti, aobotomole'nu Bouwisi, Masiya, Ngene. 12La tene ndembe ene etoluwise'nu: Otoanyai wainenge alingilomu la bolekani la alalisomu ta elelo lo nama.13Ta mbile ekabekaka bokitomo, futuku botuku bo bakitomo bokatomowa ta lobola ani bato simola Mungu, bo mbo: 14Bofoka boya Mungu ta bilela bieti likolo, la kimia ta nyele aiteni ii baito bee aomali libo.15Ta mbile bakitomo bakafowa mbo bainowe ta lobola, batingani ba bekoko bakabeka amoi mbisa ii buto linde: Tokei teee kota Beteleme, la ye tokaena taye ikaya, yeke Ngene akaenise'su. 16Bakaka mangu: La baka anya Maliya la Yosefu, la wana alali se ta elelo lo bekoko.17Mbisa ioena, bakasemowa itotina ye ikabekomoko loka lo wana oko. 18Batotina beke baokaka bambakata limwali loka lo mbeko ye ibekaka batingani ba bekoko. 19Maliya akakuka mbeko ye itotina ta botema. 20La batingani ba bekoko bakainowa la liomali la ochwela Mungu beele ta itotina ye ikaena bo la ikaoka bo, la bakabeisa beke ba beisaka bo.21Basio bonanei, beke wana aenganisaka lotonemo itombe, bakaengana, bakafa ye lina mbo Yesu, lina lie bokitomo abekaka afaka ye felo mbo inango a'anye lieme.22La, mbile ee basiyo ba lia'anisomu likaenganisomo, kotutana la bikela biya Mose, Yosefu la Maliya bakatola ye ta Yelusaleme, mbo batike wana ambe Ngene. 23Otutana la ye isonomu, ta bikela biya Ngene batolome batotina ba felofelo batofomo la Ngene. 24La lifaefi li wana a ngombe, kee bana baele ba ɛkuku, ani esonomu yo ta bikela biya Ngene.25La tene, imbaka ta Yelusaleme, buto amoi akumo Simeona, buto oko ambaka buto botanu la liengani, Mungu abekaka mbo aitongba sakolo koane Bouwisi Masiya Yesu. 26Abeisomoko la Bolomo boya Mungu mbo aimbongba ko aene Masiya Ngene.27Akandjomu la Bolimo ta esondjamelo. La, ani baboti binde batolab wana Yesu mbo baenganise tokpelo to ekela. 28Akatola ye ta boo winde, akasimola Mungu ye mbo: 29Kanikani, Ngene, tikaka bokowa wao mbo ake la kimia, kotutana la baoi bao.30Lokabaiso bange bafoena Bouwesi, 31liuwisi lie likakomio mbo liouwisa baito batotina. 32Liangangi mbo liangange ta yese itotina, la liangangi li Isalaele, baito bao.33Ise inde la inango bambaka ta limwali ani etobeka bo linde yo mbeko. 34Simeona akautcha ye, la akabeka la Maliya inango: Tene wana ono ambe botchakolomu mbo aesi lokpeso, la loemaliso baito babule ba Isalaele. 35La ambowe angene: Akpaka atotoba botema wao, mbo baenyeli ba baito babule la betema beuwe.36Imbaka aleko la botondoli amoi, Ana, boseka aya Penuele, ta bofindi boya Azele, ambaka botoolo la miaka br bule, auwaka la bolome inde miaka bosambale, kota boseka winde. 37Akatikala ndoena bolome, la miaka tuku bonanei, aitifowa ta mbele eya Mungu, la akelaka likuwa liokuela Mungu lotio la lowa la lokilio bieka la basomboli. 38Kota elo mbile akaya, akalengila Mungu kelekele, la akabeka loka loya Yesu, ta itotina ye ikaoka ye ta Yelusaleme.39Ta mbile ekaenganisaka bo, yeke ikabeka Ngene ta bikela, Yosefu la Maliya bakainowa ta Galilaya ta Nasalete, boki wibo. 40Wana akakonda la bofoka, la ayali fuu la weli, la ngbengbele eya Mungu embaka linde.41Baboti baya Yesu ba'kaka-kaka kila mwaka ta Yelusaleme, ta fete ee lieti. 42Kola ongoma embaka ye la miaka liou la yele akaka kotutana la asili ibo. 43Ongoma, ta mbile basio bakaeta, la bakainowa, wana Yesu akatikala ta Yelusaleme; ise inde la inango inde baiti luwa mbo aotika. 44Bato enyela mbo ta bolenga bo lokendo, bakakela lisio limoi la ndole, la bakaluka ye ta befindi bibo, la baluwani.45Kola ongoma, baiti anya ye, bakabinowa ta Yelusaleme okaluka ye. 46Ta basio basoso, bakafoeta, baka anya ye ta esondjamelo angali se aiteni ii baekesi ba bikela batolaila ye la bato fela fela ye felo. 47Batotina bee baokaka ye bakaumomo la baoi binde. Loka lo weli winde, la bamutoli binde.48Mbile ba ise binde baka'anya ye bakamba la limwali. inango inde, ee mbo: Wana bofelo bo'ingi o'okela ani? Tene, ise ao lange totolingowe la ngandi. 49Akabeka mbo: Otolingeni bofelo bo boingi? Oiluwilei mbo iyengani mbo lokelo mbeko iya Afa? 50Baiti lembela yeke abekaka libo.51Kola ongoma, akaka libo bolenga bomoi ta Nasaleta, la akalitisa inango inde, akaisa yeb itotina ta botema. 52La Yesu akondaka la weli. La lilatemi, la ngbengbele felo iya Mungu la felo ii baito.
